What is the difference between AI coaching and human coaching?

Both can give a solo founder a good answer. The difference is what sits around the answer: who is on the other end, when they are available, and what they know about your business.

When does a human coach win?

  • You want introductions to investors, customers or peers.
  • You want someone who knows you to hold you accountable week after week.
  • Your problem is a team, a cofounder or your own leadership, not the product's business model.
  • You want the judgment of someone who has built and sold a company.

When does an AI business coach win?

  • You need a recommendation on a Tuesday night, not a slot next Thursday.
  • Your question is specific: what to charge, where your trial signups stop, which channel to repeat.
  • You want the same framework applied to every question, with your product details already loaded.
  • You would rather pay a flat monthly price than book and pay for each session. See how a GhostCoach session works.

Can you use both?

Yes. A human coach suits the relationship and the network. Marcus suits the decisions in between. Bring Marcus the pricing question before the call, and use the call for what only a person can do.

Which one should a solo SaaS founder start with?

  • Before launch: start with Marcus. Your risk is building and pricing without talking to customers, and a recommendation costs less than a coach. See what to do after vibe coding.
  • Just launched, few paying customers: start with Marcus to find the bottleneck. Add a human coach if the bottleneck turns out to be introductions or confidence, not the funnel.
  • Growing or stuck: use both. Marcus for the pricing, churn and channel decisions. A human coach for the leadership and network questions the Ghost OS does not cover. See GhostCoach for solopreneurs.

Questions about AI and human coaching

Is an AI business coach as good as a human coach?

It depends on the job. An AI business coach gives you a structured recommendation on demand. A human coach gives you a relationship, a network and the judgment of their own experience. Marcus does not replace a lawyer, an accountant or a person who knows you.

How much does a human business coach cost compared with GhostCoach?

Human coach prices are set by each coach and vary. The price row in the table above links to the full breakdown. GhostCoach costs $79/mo for Builder and $99/mo for Operator.

Can an AI business coach replace a human coach?

For some decisions, yes: pricing, acquisition channels, trial conversion and churn. Not for introductions, accountability to a person, or team and leadership questions.

Does Marcus remember me between sessions?

Marcus starts every session from your saved business profile. It also receives a summary of your recent sessions, so it builds on what you already decided.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Both plans start with a 14-day free trial and full access to the plan you choose. A card is required to start, and if you cancel before day 15 you pay nothing.
